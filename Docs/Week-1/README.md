# Week 1 - Research Fundamentals (Academic Papers & Preliminary Proposal)

## Overview 
Week 1 focused on investigating the use of artificial intelligence in emergency department triage and developing a research proposal for a potential AI-assisted decision-support system within a Caribbean healthcare setting. 

## Learning Objectives
Week 1 focused on developing research literacy and evidence-based problem formulation through:

- Conducting a literature review of recent research on AI and machine learning applications in emergency department triage.
- Investigating emergency department workflow challenges within Caribbean healthcare systems.
- Identifying gaps in current research, particularly the limited evaluation of AI triage models in Caribbean clinical environments.
- Developing a preliminary research proposal titled Enhancing Emergency Department Triage with Artificial Intelligence.
